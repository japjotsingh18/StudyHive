# Roadmap

StudyHang is in Sprint 1 and remains pre-alpha. The canonical outcome-based roadmap is [docs/07-roadmap.md](docs/07-roadmap.md).

Current sequence:

1. Product and governance approval.
2. Contributor foundation.
3. Identity and academic graph.
4. Session coordination core.
5. Smart RSVP and notifications.
6. Live attendance and reliability.
7. Private pilots.
8. Public beta, then 1.0.

Chat and notes are public-beta candidates. AI capabilities are Phase 2 and require separate product, privacy, rights, evaluation, and cost reviews.
